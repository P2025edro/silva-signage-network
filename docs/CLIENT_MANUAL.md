# Business portal guide

Silva Signage Network shows your adverts on screens in other local businesses, and network adverts on the screens in your premises.

## Signing in

Silva Solutions creates your account and gives you a temporary password. Change it under **Settings → Your sign-in**, and turn on two-factor authentication there too.

## 1. Add your establishment

**Establishments & screens → Add establishment.** Enter the address and Eircode, choose the venue type and the zones you're in. Tick *Only show adverts during opening hours* and fill in your hours if you want screens to show adverts only while open.

**Rules** on each establishment lets you block categories (for example, a barbershop blocking other barbershops) or allow only certain categories.

## 2. Pair a TV

1. On the TV, open the web browser and go to the Silva player address you were given.
2. The TV shows an 8-character code.
3. In the portal: **Pair a screen**, type the code, choose the establishment and give the screen a name.

The TV starts playing within seconds. Leave the browser open, and turn off any screen saver or automatic power-off. Your plan sets how many screens you can pair.

## 3. Create a campaign

1. **Media library → Upload** a JPEG, PNG, WebP or MP4. Use 16:9 at 1920×1080. Videos up to 60 seconds; they play without sound.
2. **Campaigns → New campaign.** Name it, set the dates (Dublin time), and add creatives. Choose zones and venue types, or leave them open to run anywhere. Optionally restrict to certain days and times.
3. **Save draft**, then **Submit for approval** (account owners only).

Silva Solutions reviews every advert. You'll see the decision under **Updates** on your overview. If changes are requested, edit and resubmit.

## 4. While it runs

- **Pause** or **Cancel** at any time from the campaign page. Online screens update within about a minute.
- **Reports** shows playback starts, completions and failures by day, location and zone, with a CSV download, and how much of your monthly playback allocation you've used.
- If your requested plays per day are more than the network can deliver right now, the report says so.

Reports count what screens report playing. They don't measure how many people saw an advert, and nothing about viewers is collected.

## Team

**Team → Add user.** *Owners* can submit campaigns, pair screens, change rules and manage the subscription. *Staff* can upload media and prepare drafts.

## Subscription

**Subscription** shows your plan, renewal date and limits. If online billing is enabled you can start or change a plan through secure Stripe checkout; otherwise contact Silva Solutions.
